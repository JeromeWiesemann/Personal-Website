About

I am a PhD student in quantum information theory at the Institute for Quantum Computing, University of Waterloo, supervised by Prof. Norbert Lütkenhaus.

My research focuses on security analyses for quantum key distribution protocols with practical devices. I aim to develop rigorous models and tools that bridge the gap between theory and practice.

I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Physics at the Technical University of Berlin and conducted an internship at ID Quantique in Geneva before starting my PhD.

Outside of research, I enjoy graphics programming and mixing physics, computation, and design.
